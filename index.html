Donnas' Big Birthday

How the costs are split:

  • Accommodation: Split by Room Usage. If the house has 10 rooms and you use 2, you pay 20% of the accommodation bill.
  • Food & Groceries: Split by Weighted Units. This ensures children and teens pay a smaller share than adults:
    Adult (1.0) • Teen (0.5) • Kid (0.25) • Heavy Hitter (2.5)
  • Activities: Split Equally per Family. Each group contributes the same amount to the shared activity fund.
